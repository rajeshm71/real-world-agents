# Employee Benefits

This document summarizes the health, dental, retirement, and other benefits available to full-time employees. Part-time employees (fewer than 30 hours per week) are eligible for a subset of these benefits as noted below.

## Health Insurance

Full-time employees are enrolled in the company PPO health plan effective the first of the month following their start date. The company covers 90% of the employee-only premium and 70% of the premium for dependents. Employees may opt into a HSA-compatible high-deductible plan during open enrollment (November of each year) if they prefer lower premiums with higher deductibles.

## Dental and Vision

Dental and vision coverage are separate plans, both 100% employer-paid for the employee. Dependent dental coverage costs $22 per pay period; dependent vision is $8 per pay period. Vision coverage includes one exam per year and $150 toward frames or contacts.

## 401(k) Retirement Plan

Employees are eligible to enroll in the company 401(k) plan on their first day. The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of salary, vested immediately. Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual maximum. Roth 401(k) contributions are supported.

## Life and Disability Insurance

The company provides basic life insurance at 1x annual salary, employer-paid. Short-term and long-term disability insurance are also employer-paid, covering 60% of salary after a 14-day and 90-day waiting period respectively.

## Wellness Stipend

All full-time employees receive $50 per month toward wellness expenses (gym membership, fitness apps, mental health apps). Reimbursement is processed monthly via the expense system with a receipt.
