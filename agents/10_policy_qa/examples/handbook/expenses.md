# Expense Reimbursement

This policy covers business expenses employees may incur and how to submit them for reimbursement. Personal expenses are never reimbursable; if in doubt, ask your manager before spending.

## Reimbursable Categories

The following categories are pre-approved:
- **Travel**: airfare (economy class for flights under 6 hours, premium economy allowed 6+ hours), rail, ground transportation, hotel (up to $300/night in most US cities, $450/night in NYC/SF).
- **Meals during travel**: up to $85 per day inclusive of tips.
- **Client entertainment**: pre-approved by your manager for the specific event; itemized receipts required.
- **Home office equipment**: covered by the separate remote-work stipend (see `remote_work.md`).
- **Professional development**: courses, conferences, books, subscriptions up to $2,000 per calendar year per employee; requires manager pre-approval for individual items above $500.
- **Wellness**: $50/month via the wellness stipend (see `benefits.md`).

## Non-Reimbursable

- Personal meals not tied to travel or client work
- Commuting between home and your regular office
- Personal upgrades on business travel (e.g. business class you paid to upgrade to)
- Alcohol beyond client entertainment
- Traffic tickets, parking violations
- Anything without a receipt over $25

## Submission Process

Submit expenses through the Expensify integration within 30 days of the expense date. Each entry requires:
1. Date
2. Amount and currency
3. Vendor
4. Category (dropdown in Expensify)
5. Receipt attached (photo or PDF)
6. Brief business purpose (one sentence)

Manager approval is required for individual items above $250 or total submissions above $1,000. Approved reimbursements are paid via the next payroll cycle.

## Corporate Card

Employees traveling more than 4 times per year or spending more than $10,000 per year on business expenses may request a corporate card, which routes directly to accounts payable and skips the reimbursement step. Contact Finance to apply.
