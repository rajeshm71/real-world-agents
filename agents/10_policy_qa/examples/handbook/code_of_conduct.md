# Code of Conduct

This code applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors at company facilities and events. Violations should be reported to your manager or the HR team; retaliation for good-faith reports is itself a violation.

## Respectful Behavior

Treat everyone at work with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and intimidation based on race, ethnicity, national origin, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, or veteran status are prohibited. This applies to in-person interactions, all electronic communication (email, chat, video calls), and any company-affiliated events.

## Reporting Harassment

If you experience or witness harassment, report it via one of three channels:
1. Direct manager
2. HR partner assigned to your team
3. Anonymous ethics hotline (extension 4321 or ethics@example.com)

Reports are investigated promptly and confidentially. Investigators do not disclose the reporter's identity except as required to conduct a fair investigation. Documented retaliation against a good-faith reporter results in termination.

## Communication Norms

- Default to writing over meetings for anything that doesn't need real-time discussion.
- Async responses are expected within one business day for internal messages; four hours for urgent tags.
- Meetings should have a written agenda and a note-taker; the note-taker publishes notes within one business day.
- Recording video meetings requires consent from all participants at the start of the recording.

## Dress Code

Business casual for client meetings and office visits. Otherwise wear what lets you do good work. Company-branded apparel is provided free on request from the People Ops team.

## Conflicts of Interest

Disclose any outside employment, board seat, or financial interest that could affect your work here to HR within 30 days of the arrangement starting. Small angel investments in unrelated companies do not need to be disclosed unless the company later becomes a customer, competitor, or vendor.
