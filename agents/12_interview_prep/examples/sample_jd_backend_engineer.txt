Senior Backend Engineer -- Payments Platform

We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our Payments
Platform team. You'll own services that process millions of dollars
in transactions daily, working closely with product and compliance.

Responsibilities:
- Design and maintain REST APIs used by internal teams and partner
  integrations, including versioning and backward-compatibility
  strategy as the API evolves.
- Own production reliability for your services: participate in an
  on-call rotation, respond to incidents, and drive postmortems that
  lead to concrete follow-up fixes.
- Work with Postgres at scale: schema design, query optimization, and
  safe migrations against tables with hundreds of millions of rows.
- Collaborate with the compliance team to ensure new features meet
  regulatory requirements (PCI-DSS, SOC 2).
- Mentor junior engineers through code review and pairing.

Requirements:
- 5+ years of backend engineering experience, with at least 2 years
  owning services in production at scale.
- Strong experience designing and evolving REST APIs.
- Hands-on production on-call experience, including writing or
  contributing to postmortems.
- Solid relational database experience (Postgres or similar).
- Experience working in a regulated or compliance-sensitive
  environment is a plus, not required.
- Comfortable mentoring less experienced engineers.

We value engineers who can explain tradeoffs clearly, not just recite
technologies. Culture fit matters as much as technical depth.
