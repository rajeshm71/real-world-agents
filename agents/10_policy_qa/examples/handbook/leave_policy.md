# Leave Policy

Company leave policies apply to all full-time employees. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis proportional to scheduled hours.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per year, which is 1.67 days per month. Accrual begins on the first day of employment. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year; unused days beyond that cap are forfeited on January 1.

PTO requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance for absences of two or more consecutive days. Same-day PTO for illness or emergencies is always permitted; log it in the time system the same day.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 sick days per calendar year, granted on January 1 (or on start date, pro-rated). Sick leave does not carry over. A doctor's note is not required for absences of three or fewer consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Birth parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave; non-birth parents receive 12 weeks. Both apply to biological, adoptive, and foster placements. Leave may be taken continuously or in two blocks within the first 12 months of the child's arrival. Employees returning from parental leave may work reduced hours (60-80%) for an additional 8 weeks at pro-rated pay, subject to manager approval.

## Bereavement Leave

Up to 5 days of paid leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, parent, sibling, child). Up to 2 days for extended family. Additional unpaid leave may be arranged with your manager.

## Jury Duty

The company pays full salary during jury duty for up to 10 business days per calendar year. Beyond that, employees may use PTO or take unpaid leave.
