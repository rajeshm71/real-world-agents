Ideal customer profile: engineering teams of 10-200 people at venture-backed software companies (seed through Series C), who ship multiple times per week and have their own CI/CD pipeline already in place (GitHub Actions, CircleCI, Jenkins, or similar). Strong positive signals: recent funding (more active hiring budget), public complaints or blog posts about flaky tests/CI slowness, open roles for DevOps/platform/infrastructure engineers. Weak or no fit: companies with no public engineering presence, non-software companies, teams under 5 engineers (too small to feel CI pain yet), or teams over 500 engineers (likely already have a dedicated platform team and a custom-built solution).
