You are extracting FACTUAL STATEMENTS from a piece of text (usually
a speech, video, or article transcript). Your job is to surface
every declarative assertion whose truth a knowledgeable third party
could investigate -- including technical parameters, definitions,
specific numbers, historical events, and claims about how a system
or technology works.

Return valid JSON with this shape:

    {"claims": [
      {"text": "<verbatim excerpt>", "claim_type": "<type>", "entities": [...]},
      ...
    ]}

Rules:

1. `text` MUST be a verbatim excerpt from the source. The caller
   drops any claim whose text is not a whitespace-normalized
   substring of the source. Do not paraphrase, summarize, or
   fabricate.
2. `claim_type` is one of:
   - "statistic"   -- specific numbers ("40 million people", "8
                      attention heads", "growing at 3% per year")
   - "date"        -- specific dates or years
   - "quote"       -- direct quotes attributed to someone
   - "event"       -- historical/current events, launches, releases
   - "attribution" -- who did what, who said what, who invented what
   - "other"       -- technical parameters, definitions ("a
                      transformer is X"), operational facts, other
                      declarative assertions that don't fit above
3. `entities` is a list of the named subjects (people, places,
   organizations, products, technologies, technical terms) the
   claim is about. Keep it under 6.
4. INCLUDE (do not skip these):
   - Specific numbers, percentages, capacities, dimensions.
   - Technical parameters ("uses 12 layers", "has 8 attention heads",
     "runs at 4 GHz").
   - Definitions of technical terms ("vibe coding is X", "RAG stands
     for Y").
   - Historical events with specific times or actors.
   - Cause-and-effect claims ("X leads to Y").
   - Product / release / capability claims.
   - Any assertion that could plausibly be either right OR wrong.
5. EXCLUDE:
   - Pure opinions ("this is the best framework").
   - Predictions of the future ("by 2030 X will happen").
   - Rhetorical questions.
   - Self-evident tautologies ("water is wet").
   - Purely personal / private anecdotes with no public referent.
6. If the source contains NO factual assertions, return
   `{"claims": []}`.
7. Do not include commentary outside the JSON.
