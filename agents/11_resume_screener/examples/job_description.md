# Senior Backend Engineer -- Paystream

Paystream is an eight-person seed-stage fintech building the settlement
layer under a handful of consumer payment apps. We are hiring our
second senior backend engineer to own the ledger service.

## What you will do

- Own the double-entry ledger service end to end: schema, write path,
  read APIs, reconciliation jobs.
- Take pager rotation once ramped (roughly a week every six weeks).
  Incidents are rare but load-bearing when they happen.
- Work closely with the two founding engineers on capacity planning as
  transaction volume grows past 100 TPS sustained.
- Write the runbooks for the on-call rotation you help staff.

## Must have

- 5+ years shipping Python in production.
- Direct experience running a service that handled real user traffic
  and real money -- payments, banking, exchange, treasury, or similar.
- Comfort with async Python, PostgreSQL under load, and event-driven
  patterns (Kafka, SQS, or equivalent).
- Distributed systems fundamentals: idempotency, exactly-once
  processing, consensus, retries, backpressure.
- History of being on-call for what you build.

## Nice to have

- Prior startup experience (seed / Series A stage).
- Familiarity with formal ledger patterns (accounting-style,
  double-entry, event-sourced).
- Open-source contribution to a database or messaging system.

## How we work

Fully remote across US and Europe time zones. Async by default with
one weekly team sync. Small team so scope changes weekly; comfort with
ambiguity is required. We do not use meetings to make decisions --
proposals live as written docs and get commented on asynchronously.
