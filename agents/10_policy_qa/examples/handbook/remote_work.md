# Remote Work Policy

Most roles at the company are eligible for remote or hybrid work. Some roles (customer-facing operations, hardware engineering) require some or all on-site work; check with your manager or the job posting for your specific role.

## Eligibility

Full-time employees whose roles are marked "remote-eligible" or "hybrid" may work remotely full-time or on any hybrid schedule agreed with their manager. New hires typically spend their first 2 weeks in-office for onboarding; this may be waived for fully-remote hires living more than 100 miles from the nearest office.

## Home Office Setup

Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time $1,500 home office stipend to cover a desk, chair, monitor, and other equipment. The stipend is available upon completion of the first 30 days. A second $500 refresh is available every 3 years. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.

Company-provided equipment includes a laptop, external monitor, keyboard, and mouse. Employees may request an ergonomic evaluation to determine additional equipment needs.

## Internet Reimbursement

Remote employees receive $50 per month toward home internet. Reimbursement is added to the paycheck automatically; no receipts required.

## Core Collaboration Hours

Regardless of timezone, all remote employees are expected to be available for real-time collaboration between 10 AM and 2 PM in their primary team's timezone. Outside those hours, async communication is the default. If your team's timezone conflicts with your personal schedule, work with your manager to define alternate core hours.

## Working from Other Countries

Employees may work from a country outside their home country for up to 30 days per calendar year without pre-approval. Longer stays require legal and tax review; contact People Ops at least 60 days before the planned start date.

## Return-to-Office

The company does not require any specific number of in-office days per week or month for remote-eligible roles. Managers may request in-office time for specific projects (team offsites, in-person interviews); such requests should give at least 2 weeks notice and are reimbursed for travel.
